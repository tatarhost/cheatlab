CHEATLAB Android — Third-party notices

The Android application source does not bundle third-party Android runtime
libraries. It uses Android platform APIs and the Android System WebView
installed on the user's device. The WebView implementation and its components
are supplied by the device platform/provider and remain subject to their own
licenses and notices.

The Android Gradle Plugin and Gradle are build-time tools only; they are not
packaged into the installed application.
